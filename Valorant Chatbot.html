IMPROVE YOUR GAME

CORE STRATEGY

Mechanical skill matters, but smart decision-making wins rounds. Build strong fundamentals before focusing on advanced plays.

01 // AIM

Crosshair Placement

Keep your crosshair at head level and pre-aim common angles before you expose yourself. Instead of reacting after an enemy appears, position your crosshair where an enemy is most likely to be. Clear angles systematically and keep your crosshair close to the next possible target so your first shot requires minimal adjustment.

02 // MOVEMENT

Movement & Peeking

Learn when to jiggle peek, shoulder peek, wide swing or hold an angle. Stop your movement before committing to accurate shots. Avoid repeatedly peeking the same angle because opponents can easily pre-aim your timing. Change your peek timing and use utility when entering dangerous areas.

03 // ATTACK

Attack Strategy

Attack with a purpose instead of simply rushing toward a site. Use utility to gain information, deny defenders and create safe areas for your team. Once space is created, trade teammates, control important positions and plant the Spike where your team can defend it effectively.

04 // DEFENSE

Defensive Play

Defending does not always mean taking the first fight. Hold useful positions, gather information and stay alive when possible. If attackers commit to another site, rotate using the information available instead of guessing. After a retake, coordinate utility and enter together rather than taking isolated fights.

05 // ECONOMY

Economy

Understand when your team can afford rifles, shields and abilities and when saving is better. Avoid buying expensive equipment alone when the rest of your team cannot match it. Coordinate force buys and saves so the entire team can reach a strong buy together on the following round.

06 // TEAMPLAY

Communication

Give short, accurate and useful callouts. Communicate enemy locations, damage, utility, Spike information and rotations. Avoid flooding voice chat during fights. Good communication gives teammates information they can immediately act on and helps the team make faster decisions.

07 // UTILITY

Use Utility With Purpose

Do not use abilities simply because they are available. Think about what each piece of utility accomplishes. Smokes can deny vision, flashes can create safe opportunities, information tools can reveal positions and defensive abilities can delay pushes. Combine abilities with teammate actions whenever possible.

08 // INFORMATION

Play Around Information

Pay attention to enemy utility, footsteps, revealed positions, missing players and Spike information. Every piece of information can change your decision. If several enemies are confirmed on one side of the map, your team can often exploit the space they have left behind.

09 // CLUTCH

Stay Calm

In clutch situations, slow the game down and make the enemy work for information. Isolate fights instead of exposing yourself to several opponents at once. Use sound and timing to create uncertainty, and remember that surviving longer can force opponents into mistakes.
